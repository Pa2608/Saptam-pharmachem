About Saptam Pharmachem

Committed to quality, reliability and lasting partnerships.

Who we are

Connecting Quality Materials with the Pharmaceutical Industry

SAPTAM PHARMACHEM is a trusted name in India's pharmaceutical trading industry, known for delivering reliable access to high-quality raw materials. We specialize in the distribution of pharmaceutical APIs, polymers, solvents, chemicals, vitamins, and plant extracts.

With a strategically advantageous location in Idar, Gujarat, we support efficient supply operations across healthcare sectors. Our commitment is to build long-term, value-driven partnerships and support innovation in the pharmaceutical space. Quality, integrity, and customer satisfaction are at the heart of everything we do.
